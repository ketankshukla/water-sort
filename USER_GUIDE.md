<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">🎯 The Goal</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
Every level gives you a set of tubes filled with <b>mixed-up colored liquid</b>. Your mission is simple to learn but tricky to master:
</p>

> [!NOTE]
> **🥅 Win condition:** Sort the liquid so that **each tube holds only one color** (or is left empty). When every color is gathered into its own tube, the level is solved! 🎉

---

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">🕹️ How to Play</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
Playing is all about <b>tapping tubes</b> to pour liquid from one to another:
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
1. 👆 <b>Tap a tube</b> to pick it up — it lifts slightly to show it's selected.<br/>
2. 👉 <b>Tap a second tube</b> to pour the top color into it.<br/>
3. 🔁 Tap the same tube again to <b>put it back down</b> and cancel.
</p>

---

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">📜 The Pouring Rules</h2>

| ✅ You CAN pour when... | ❌ You CANNOT pour when... |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🎯 The colors **match** — the top color of your tube matches the top color of the target | 🚫 The top colors are **different** |
| 🫗 The target tube is **empty** | 🪣 The target tube is **full** |
| 📦 There's **enough room** for at least one block | 🧊 The target is **frozen/locked** (see below) |

> [!TIP]
> 💡 When you pour, **all matching blocks of the top color move together** — as many as will fit. Plan around this to move stacks efficiently!

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">🧰 Your Toolbar</h2>

| Button | What it does |
| :---: | :--- |
| ↩️ **Undo** | Take back your last pour — use it as much as you like |
| ➕ **Add tube** | Drop in an extra empty tube when you need more breathing room |
| 💡 **Hint** | Stuck for ideas? Get a suggested next move highlighted |
| ⏮️ **Level 1** | Restart your progress from the very first level |
| 🔊 **Sound** | Tap the speaker icon (top-right) to mute or unmute |

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">🧊 Special Tubes</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
As you climb the levels, some tubes get <b>special rules</b> that add a twist. Don't worry — the game guarantees every level is still solvable! 🛡️
</p>

| Tube | Emoji | Rule |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Frozen** | 🧊 | Sealed in ice — it **rejects all pours** until you pour a *matching* color onto it once, cracking the ice open. Appears from **Level 2**. |
| **One-Way** | ➡️ | Liquid can **only pour out**, never in — until you fully empty it, then it becomes a normal tube. Appears from **Level 4**. |
| **Locked** | 🔒 | Sealed shut until an unlock condition is met (a number of moves, tubes completed, etc.). Watch its **live counter**. Appears from **Level 6**. |
| **Hazard** | ⚡ | A random tube may *temporarily* freeze, lock, or go one-way **mid-game** — then auto-lifts after a few moves. |

> [!WARNING]
> ⚡ **Dynamic hazards are always fair.** They only appear if a legal move still exists, they lift automatically, and they can **never** be the reason you're stuck.

---

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">😌 You Can't Get Permanently Stuck</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
This is the friendliest Water Sort around! 💚 The game <b>watches every move</b> and quietly blocks any pour that would make the puzzle impossible to finish. If a tube flashes and nothing happens, that move would have trapped you — just try a different one.
</p>

> [!TIP]
> 🧠 Combined with **Undo** and **Hint**, this means you can always find your way to a win. Experiment freely!

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">🧠 Tips &amp; Strategy</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
🥇 <b>Free up a tube early.</b> Try to fully clear one color into a single tube as soon as you can — it gives you room to maneuver.<br/><br/>
🌊 <b>Pour into matching colors</b> before dumping into empty tubes. Empties are precious!<br/><br/>
🔭 <b>Look ahead.</b> Before a big pour, check what color is hiding underneath — you don't want to bury a color you need.<br/><br/>
↩️ <b>Undo is your friend.</b> Try a line of moves; if it doesn't pan out, walk it back and try another.<br/><br/>
💡 <b>Use Hint sparingly</b> for that "aha!" nudge when you're truly stuck.
</p>

<h2 style="font-family:'Segoe UI',Verdana,sans-serif;">❓ Quick FAQ</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
<b>Q: I tapped a tube and nothing happened — why?</b><br/>
A: Either the colors didn't match, the target was full/sealed, or the move would have made the puzzle unsolvable. Try another tube! 🔄
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
<b>Q: Can I lose?</b><br/>
A: There's no "game over" — you can always undo, add a tube, or restart. The only goal is to relax and solve. 😌
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem; line-height:1.7;">
<b>Q: Will my progress be saved?</b><br/>
A: Yes! Your level, score, and current board are saved on your device automatically. 💾
</p>
